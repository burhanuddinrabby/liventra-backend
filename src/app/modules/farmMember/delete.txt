Model 03: FarmMember API Endpoints

Method Endpoint Access Level Description
POST /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members Farm Owner / Manager Adds/invites a registered user as a member of the farm with a specific role.
GET /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members Farm Member Lists all active members and staff assigned to the specified farm.
GET /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId Farm Member Retrieves detailed profile metadata for a specific farm member.
PUT /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId Farm Owner / Manager Updates a member's operational details (role, salary, nid, joiningDate).
PATCH /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId/toggle-active Farm Owner / Manager Activates or deactivates a member's access (isActive: true/false) without deleting historical data.
DELETE /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId Farm Owner Soft-deletes or removes a member relationship from the farm.


Endpoint Descriptions & Business Logic

1. Onboarding & Member Association

POST /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members
Associates a user with a farm by creating a FarmMember record. The payload specifies the target user (userId), role (Owner, Manager, Worker, Veterinarian, Accountant), nid, joiningDate, and optional salary. Prevent duplicate active memberships for the same user-farm combination.

2. Member Retrieval & Directory

GET /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members
Returns the list of personnel associated with the farm. Essential for UI staff management dashboards, assigning tasks, and filtering operational activities (e.g., who logged a feeding or milking session).
GET /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId
Retrieves comprehensive information for a single farm member, including their farm-specific role, employment dates, and national identity details (nid).

3. Role & Salary Management

PUT /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId
Updates farm-level permissions or operational parameters. For example, promoting a Worker to Manager or updating salary information. Restrict role escalation (e.g., a Manager cannot promote themselves or others to Owner).

4. Deactivation & Lifecycle Control

PATCH /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId/toggle-active
Deactivates a user's membership (isActive: false) when they leave employment or change farms. Deactivation revokes their ability to perform farm operations immediately while preserving historical logs (e.g., feedings performed or treatments administered by them remain traceable).
DELETE /api/v1/farms/:farmId/members/:memberId
Removes a member entry. Used primarily for cleanups of accidental invitations or unconfirmed entries; deactivation is preferred for active workers with historical operational records.
